How to bypass the Spirit in emergency situations

Temporary bypass routing feed water from the prefilters directly to the control boxes.

!
Before you start

Have a dry rag at hand for wiping up water — some water will escape when tubes are disconnected.

  1. 1
    Shut off mains, turn off the booster pump & disconnect the Spirit
    Close the feed valve right where the mains are connected.
  2. 2
    Disconnect the 3/8″ tube at the Spirit inlet
    This is the tube going from the prefilters to the Spirit.
  3. 3
    Disconnect the tube going into the Y-connector
    The Y-connector is the one that leads to the control boxes.
  4. 4
    Connect the prefilter tubing directly into the Y-connector
    The tubing from the prefilters now feeds the Y-connector that supplies the control boxes, bypassing the Spirit.
  5. 5
    Turn on the booster pump and mains
    Open the feed valve, switch the booster pump back on and check all connections for leaks.
