# Macedonia Coffee Market — Knowledge Note

> Background research for the HORECA / Café Station 1™ context in North Macedonia.
> Primary source: European Coffee Trip — "Barista Stories: Jerry Kaandorp."

## Jerry Kaandorp & Jeronimo Specialty Coffee

- **Who:** Jerry Kaandorp — owner, barista, roaster & mentor at **Jeronimo Specialty Coffee**.
- **Where:** Operates between **Skopje (North Macedonia)** and **Aalsmeer (Netherlands)**.
- **Story:** Jerry met his wife, Ivona, in the Netherlands and "fell for her and the Balkans
  at the same time." Over 32 years of marriage he built a life that eventually led him to open
  a specialty coffee roastery in Skopje.
- **Key lesson:** Running Jeronimo across two markets taught him that **coffee culture is always
  local, even when the coffee itself is universal.** In North Macedonia coffee is woven into
  hospitality and tradition in a way that differs from the Dutch market.
- **Growth:** Jeronimo is scaling up — working on **franchises across Macedonia**.

## North Macedonia Coffee Culture

- **Ottoman heritage:** Turkish coffee was introduced by merchants ~15th century during the
  Ottoman Empire. Coffee is a fundamental part of social interaction and daily life — not just
  a drink.
- **Traditional Macedonian coffee (Tursko Kafe / "Turkish coffee"):** The most culturally
  significant brew. Strong, unfiltered, made with very finely ground (almost powder) Arabica,
  water, and optional sugar. Similar to versions across the Balkans and the Middle East.
- **Social ritual:** Cafés are central to North Macedonian social life, especially in the
  capital, Skopje.

## Specialty Coffee Scene (Skopje)

- The specialty scene has been building over the last decade and accelerating in recent years.
- Notable specialty players in Skopje include:
  - **Jeronimo Specialty Coffee**
  - **Dabov Specialty Coffee Macedonia**
  - **EDGE Specialty Coffee**
  - **Lusso Specialty Coffee**
  - **The Dude Specialty Coffee**
- Modern coffee bars range from espresso and signature brews to experimental drinks
  (e.g. peanut-butter-flavoured brews, Ethiopian espresso), reflecting both local and
  global tastes.

## Relevance to HORECA / Café Station 1™

- The Macedonia market mixes **deeply rooted traditional café culture** with a **fast-growing
  specialty segment** — both rely heavily on in-café equipment uptime.
- Expansion-minded operators (like Jeronimo's franchise push) are a natural fit for a
  self-service troubleshooting support tool: consistent equipment reliability across multiple
  locations matters when scaling a brand.
- Any localization effort should respect the strong local hospitality/tradition framing; coffee
  service is a social ritual, not just a transaction.
